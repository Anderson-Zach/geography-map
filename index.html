About This Visualization

This interactive heatmap visualizes the density and intensity of aerial bombing operations across the European theater. The "hotter" the color (transitioning toward red), the higher the total tonnage of explosives dropped in that specific area during the selected timeframe. Zoom in or pan around to view city and country names, and use the playback controls to watch the campaign unfold over time.

Note on the Hover Tool: The mouse tooltip provides a localized estimate by searching a roughly 15-kilometer radius around your cursor. Because of overlapping target coordinates, imperfect historical logging, and spatial rounding, this number is a rough approximation of the regional intensity rather than an exact historical record for a single pinpoint target.

Data Source: THOR (Theater History of Operations Reports) Database, compiled by the US Air Force Research Institute.
